🎮 Gaming Optimization

The gaming section focuses on three things: keeping the processor free for your game, reducing unnecessary background chatter, and making sure your graphics settings match your hardware. Selecting a game profile adjusts power settings so your PC does not throttle during long sessions, and it trims background tasks that compete for memory and processing time.

You can save a separate profile for each game you play regularly. Switching between profiles takes a single click, and the toolkit remembers your choice the next time you launch it.

📈 FPS Tracking and Benchmarks

The FPS tracker displays a small overlay while you play, showing the current frame rate, the average, and the lowest value recorded during your session. After you finish, the toolkit stores a summary so you can compare one game session against another or check whether a change actually helped.

Benchmarks test your processor, memory, and drive in sequence and give each a score. Scores are most useful when compared on the same machine before and after changes, which is exactly how the toolkit presents them.

🧹 Startup Review and Storage Analysis

Startup programs have a big effect on how quickly your PC becomes usable after you log in. The startup review lists each entry with an impact rating, its publisher, and how long it typically takes to load. Toggling an entry off does not delete the program — it simply stops it from opening automatically, and you can turn it back on at any time.

The storage analysis scans your drives and shows a breakdown by folder, so you can see instantly whether your space is going to games, videos, downloads, or system files. Temporary file cleanup is included and is safe to run regularly.

🛠️ Troubleshooting
